# Publisher source data

Chen, X., Yang, D., Li, Z. et al. *Laser-induced nucleation of magnetic hopfions*. Nature Physics 22, 736–744 (2026). The two XLSX attachments are preserved byte for byte, with their original filenames. The receipt records the official figure labels, separate download URLs and matching SHA-256 digests. [Article and rights](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-026-03236-0) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The energy columns and published figures use a pJ label. Each worksheet energy formula divides density times the simulation volume by 10⁻¹⁵. The density columns omit the export units. The audit records both resulting physical scales.
